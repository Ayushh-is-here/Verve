Battery Whitelist Guide

Modern Android operating systems (Samsung One UI, Google Pixel, Xiaomi HyperOS) aggressively kill background tasks. To guarantee 100% wake-up reliability:

  1. Go to Android Settings, Apps, Verve
  2. Tap Battery and select Unrestricted
  3. Allow Autostart in OEM power management menus
  4. Exclude Verve from Deep Sleeping Apps
